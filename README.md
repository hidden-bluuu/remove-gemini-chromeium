# No Google AI

Removes Google AI Overviews and AI Mode from Google Search.

## Install
1. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode**.
2. Click **Load unpacked** and pick this folder.

## Features
- **Web tab method**: searches are sent to Google's "Web" results (`udm=14`), which have no AI Overviews.
- **Hide method**: keeps the normal results page and hides the AI Overview box.
- Hide the AI Mode tab, ads, and "People also ask".
- Force an English Google interface (`hl=en`).
- Popup: on/off switch, counter, and "Show AI for this search".
- Shortcut Alt+Shift+A toggles the extension (change it at `chrome://extensions/shortcuts`).
- Settings sync across your Chrome profile.
